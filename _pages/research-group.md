Our research group at Augusta University studies the mathematical foundations of machine learning and optimization, with an emphasis on reliable learning and decision-making under uncertainty.

## Research Areas

- **Reinforcement learning and distributed learning:** stochastic approximation, multi-agent and federated learning, meta-learning, asynchronous updates, and Byzantine robustness.
- **Minimax and submodular optimization:** algorithms for continuous and discrete decisions, with convergence and approximation guarantees.
- **Sequential inference and quickest change detection:** unnormalized models, likelihood-free methods, and monitoring of multiple data streams.
- **Generative models and language-model inference:** diffusion-based sampling, in-context generation, and game-theoretic decoding.
- **Certified robustness:** convex relaxations and efficient verification methods for neural networks.
- **Information theory and entropy estimation:** sample complexity and statistical limits for Rényi entropy and min-entropy.

For examples of our work, see the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AikTZ-AAAAAJ&hl=en).
