Our research group at Augusta University studies the mathematical foundations of machine learning and optimization, with an emphasis on reliable learning and decision-making under uncertainty.

## Research Areas

- Stochastic approximation and reinforcement learning.
- Distributed and federated learning, including asynchronous and Byzantine settings.
- Minimax and submodular optimization.
- Sequential change detection and statistical inference for unnormalized models.

For examples of our work, see the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AikTZ-AAAAAJ&hl=en).
